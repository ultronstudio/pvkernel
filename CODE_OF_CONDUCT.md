# Code of Conduct

pvKernel is a technical, early-stage systems project. This code of conduct
describes the standard of behavior expected in repository discussions,
issues, pull requests, and any official pvKernel community spaces.

## Our commitment

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, professional, and technically
focused community. Everyone participating in pvKernel spaces is expected to
treat other participants with courtesy, regardless of experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and assume good faith.
- Focus criticism on code, design decisions, evidence, and observed behavior,
  not on a person's worth, identity, or competence in general.
- Disagree on technical matters openly; technical disagreement is a normal
  and healthy part of kernel development.
- Provide constructive, specific feedback, especially in code review.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, direction, and priorities, even
  when you disagree with them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation of any participant.
- Discriminatory remarks or jokes based on personal characteristics.
- Doxxing or publishing others' private information without consent.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language directed at a person
  rather than their contribution.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Using the private security-reporting channel for ordinary interpersonal
  disagreements instead of its intended purpose.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to pvKernel's GitHub repository (issues, pull
requests, discussions, and comments) and any other space that is officially
designated as a pvKernel community space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this code
of conduct. Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict a person's
participation in pvKernel spaces when necessary.

Enforcement decisions take into account context, severity, prior history,
and the safety of other participants. There is no guaranteed response time
for reports or enforcement actions.

## Reporting

Reports of code of conduct violations may be sent privately to
contact@petrvurm.cz. Please do not use this channel to report suspected
security vulnerabilities; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for that process,
and do not use the security-reporting channel for ordinary interpersonal
disagreements.
